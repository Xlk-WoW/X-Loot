  X-Loot

A clean loot browser and stat-based WishList for WoW 3.3.5a. Browse every dungeon and raid drop from Classic to Cataclysm, and find the best gear for your build in seconds. Open it with /xl.

    Features:

 Four expansions: Classic, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King and Cataclysm, each with its dungeons, raids and bosses.

 Every difficulty: Normal, Heroic, 10 / 25 Man and Mythic, with the real item level of each version.

 Mythic dungeon ranks: browse M0 and M12, or list every rank from M1 to M12 at 0/8 and 8/8 in the WishList.

 WishList: tick the stats you want (Spell Power, Spirit, Haste, Strength, Stamina and more) and get every matching item, highest item level first.

 Filters: narrow results by gear slot, content type, Mythic rank and progression phase.

 Priority sorting: put the highest Stamina, Intellect, Spell Power, Strength or Agility first.

 Saved scan cache: every item is scanned once and remembered, so the WishList loads instantly in later sessions.

 Item search: find any item by name.

 Item actions: hover for the full tooltip, Shift-click to link in chat, Ctrl-click to preview in the dressing room.

 Clean dark interface: flat orange-accented design, draggable window and minimap button.

 One command: /xl opens and closes the addon.
